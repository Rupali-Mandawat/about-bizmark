ABOUT BIZMARK

BizMark is the flagship event of DCG, blending creativity, strategic thinking and business insights. As a national-level competition, it attracts participants from across India for diverse contests, engaging panel discussions, and networking sessions with industry leaders. BizMark'25 offers an inspiring, innovative, and competitive environment for all attendees.

ABOUT DTU CONSULTING GROUP

DTU Consulting Group (DCG) is a premier society at Delhi Technological University, renowned for fostering consulting skills and problem solving expertise among students. By bridging the gap between academic knowledge and industry practices, DCG provides a platform for experiential learning through events, workshops and live projects.
